## 3. Persistent Per-Client Data Usage Limit (Quota)
- **Data Model & Validation (`DataQuota.kt`, `ClientManager.kt`)**:
  - Added `dataLimitBytes: Long` (`0L` = Unlimited; `1 MB = 1,048,576 bytes`; `1 GB = 1024 MB`), `quotaResetPeriod: QuotaResetPeriod` (`DAILY`, `WEEKLY`, `MONTHLY`, `MANUAL` — default `MANUAL`), `quotaUsedRxBytes: Long`, `quotaUsedTxBytes: Long`, `quotaWindowId: String`, `quotaWindowStartEpochMs: Long`, `quotaLastUpdatedEpochMs: Long`, and `quotaResetGeneration: Long` to `ClientRule`, `ConnectedClient`, and `DiscoveredHost`.
  - Backward-compatible JSON parsing in `ClientRule.parseList`: legacy rules from `v0.10.1` and earlier load cleanly with `dataLimitBytes = 0L` (Unlimited) and `QuotaResetPeriod.MANUAL`.
- **Userspace Datapath Accounting & Atomic Quota Enforcement (`TunTrafficEngine.kt`, `HostFirewall.kt`)**:
  - Tracks attributed per-MAC download (`usedRxBytes`) and upload (`usedTxBytes`) bytes in `TunTrafficEngine` for both direct client IP matches and post-NAT `192.0.2.2` flows resolved via BPF conntrack (`BpfFlowAttribution`) or `/proc/net/nf_conntrack`.
  - **Single-Counting Guarantee**: Forwarded packets accepted into `ClientPacingQueue` (`PacketDecision.RATE_LIMIT`) are charged once upon acceptance and are **not** double-counted when drained by `drainDuePacedPackets`. If a queued packet is later dropped due to queue overflow (`recordQueueOverflowDrop`), client disconnect, session stop, or policy block, its pre-charged bytes are refunded via `refundQuotaBytes`.
  - **No False Charging**: Blocked/dropped packets and unattributed post-NAT `192.0.2.2` packets (`matchedMac == null`) are never charged to any client's quota.
  - **Immediate Quota Block & Manual Block Independence**: When `usedRxBytes + usedTxBytes >= dataLimitBytes`, the client's policy is atomically marked `isBlocked = true` with `blockReason = "Data limit reached"` in `TunTrafficEngine` and `HostFirewall` without mutating the administrator's persistent `ClientRule.isBlocked` flag. Manual admin block (`"Blocked by admin"`) always takes priority over `"Data limit reached"`.
- **Checkpointed Persistence & Window Rollover (`DataQuota.kt`, `TunTrafficEngine.kt`, `SettingsStore.kt`, `SessionService.kt`)**:
  - Throttled atomic checkpointing (`shizzi_client_quotas_v1.json`) flushes at most every 5 seconds or 256 KB of attributed traffic, and immediately on quota exhaustion, rule updates, manual reset, or `TunTrafficEngine.stop()` / `clearAll()`.
  - `SessionService` synchronizes live service quota counters back into `SettingsStore` (`client_rules_json`) so usage and quota enforcement survive screen navigation, hotspot/VPN restarts, service restarts, and app force-stop/relaunch.
  - Lazy local-timezone window rollover (`DAILY` at `00:00`, `WEEKLY` on Monday `00:00`, `MONTHLY` on the 1st at `00:00`, `MANUAL` never auto-resets) with high-water `windowId` comparison to protect against backward wall-clock shifts and DST transitions.
- **UI Configuration & Manual Reset (`ClientsPage.kt`)**:
  - Added **Data Usage Limit (MB)** input (`data_limit_mb_input`), **Quota Reset Period** chips (`quota_period_daily`, `quota_period_weekly`, `quota_period_monthly`, `quota_period_manual`), live period usage & next-reset summary (`quota_usage_summary_text`, `quota_next_reset_text`), and **Reset Usage** button (`reset_quota_usage_button`) with confirmation dialog (`confirm_reset_quota_button`) to `ConfigureClientDialog`.
  - Added quota status badge (`Data: <used> / <limit> (<pct>%) • <rem> left` or `Limit reached`) and attribution status line (`client_quota_attribution_<MAC>`) to `ClientCard` for both active and offline saved rules.
